Official files: Brown's Office of University Communications, "Visual Identity
Policy and Standards" (ouc.brown.edu → Logos & Identity). Community members
download the primary logo (PNG/EPS) from the Visual Identity Adobe Library;
Brown login required. Convert EPS to SVG or PDF-embeddable PNG before use.

The Graduate School's guidelines do not call for a logo on the title page;
leave it off the submitted version unless told otherwise.
